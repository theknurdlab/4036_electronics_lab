Amplifier Equations & Component Effects Small-Signal AC Analysis
  • Mid-Band Voltage Gain & dB Gain:
    Av = VoVin = − RC ∥ RLre   Gain (dB) = 20 log10 |Av|
  • Bandwidth & 3-dB Cutoff Frequencies:
    BW = fH − fL  (Gain drops by 3 dB or to 0.707 Av(mid) at fL and fH)
  • Effect of Removing Bypass Capacitor CE: Without CE, emitter resistor RE introduces current-series negative feedback, reducing mid-band gain to Av ≈ −(RC ∥ RL) / RE while widening bandwidth.
