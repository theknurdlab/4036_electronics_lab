Characteristic Equation & Race-Around Note
• Characteristic Equation: Q(t+1) = J · Q̄(t) + K̄ · Q(t)
• Race-Around Condition: When J = K = 1 and the level-triggered clock pulse width is longer than the gate propagation delay, Q toggles continuously. Always use the single-pulse (debounced) switch on the trainer kit.
