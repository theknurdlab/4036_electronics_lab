  • Virtual Ground & Phase Inversion: Non-inverting Pin 3 is grounded (V3 = 0 V), holding inverting Pin 2 at virtual ground (V2 ≈ 0 V). Consequently, Zin = Ri and output Vo is inverted by 180°.
