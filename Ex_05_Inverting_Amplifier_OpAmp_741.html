Design Calculation & Virtual Ground Analysis IC 741C Closed-Loop
  • Closed-Loop Voltage Gain:
    Av = VoVin = − RfRi = − 10.0 kΩ1.0 kΩ = −10.00
  • Virtual Ground & Phase Inversion: Non-inverting Pin 3 is grounded (V3 = 0 V), holding inverting Pin 2 at virtual ground (V2 ≈ 0 V). Consequently, Zin = Ri and output Vo is inverted by 180°.
  • IC 741 Power & Output Pins: Pin 7 = +VCC (+15 V), Pin 4 = −VEE (−15 V), Pin 6 = Output Vo.
