Design Equations & Oscilloscope Grounding Safety
  • Firing Angle & Time Delay:
    tα = α360° × T = α360° × 20 ms
  • Average DC Load Voltage:
    Vdc = Vm2π (1 + cos α)  where Vm = 12√2 ≈ 16.97 V
  • Safe Oscilloscope Probing: Both DSO ground clips connect to the SCR Cathode (common reference). CH1 measures Vs and CH2 measures Vak; load voltage VL = Vs − Vak is obtained using DSO Channel Math (CH1 − CH2) to prevent shorting the SCR through earth ground.
