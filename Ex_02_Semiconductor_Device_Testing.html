DMM Testing Rules & Diagnostic Criteria Diode-Test Mode
  • PN & Zener Diodes (1N4007, 5.1V Zener): Forward bias (Red → Anode, Black → Cathode) reads 0.55 V – 0.72 V; reverse bias reads OL (open). The silver/black band marks the Cathode (K).
  • NPN Transistor (BC548, 1:C 2:B 3:E): With Red on Base (B) and Black on E or C, both B–E and B–C junctions read 0.60 V – 0.72 V. C–E reads OL in both directions.
  • SCR (TYN612, 1:K 2:A 3:G): Gate-Cathode (Red → G, Black → K) conducts like a PN junction (~0.65 V or low resistance); A–K blocks (OL) in both polarities without gate trigger.
  • UJT (2N2646, TO-18 Tab = E): Inter-base resistance B1–B2 is 4 kΩ – 10 kΩ (reads OL in diode range); Red → Emitter (E) to Black → B1 or B2 reads 0.60 V – 0.75 V.
