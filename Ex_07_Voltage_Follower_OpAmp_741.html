• 100% Voltage-Series Negative Feedback: Output Pin 6 is wired directly to Inverting Pin 2 (β = 1), forcing Vo to track Vi identically in amplitude and phase (0°).
• Why Output Does Not Drop with RL = 1 kΩ: Because of its ultra-high input impedance (~2 MΩ) and very low closed-loop output impedance (<1 Ω), the buffer isolates high-impedance sources from low-impedance loads.
• Try "Without Buffer": Toggle Buffer Mode to observe how a 10 kΩ sensor/source collapses to 0.18 Vpp across a 1 kΩ load without the voltage follower!
