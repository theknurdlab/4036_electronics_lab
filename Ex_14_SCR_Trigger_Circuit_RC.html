RC Phase-Control Principle & Design Equations
  • Charging & Reset Diodes: During the positive half-cycle, capacitor C charges through R1 + R2; when VC reaches VD1 + VGT (≈ 1.5 V), the SCR fires at α (0° to 180°). During the negative half-cycle, diode D2 resets C to −0.7 V so every cycle starts identically.
  • Firing Delay & Average Load Voltage:
    tα = α360° × 20 ms   Vdc = Vm2π (1 + cos α)  where Vm = 12√2 ≈ 16.97 V.
  • Safe DSO Channel Subtraction: Load voltage VL = Vs − Vak is measured via CH1 − CH2 with both DSO probe ground clips connected to the common cathode.
