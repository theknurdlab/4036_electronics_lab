Trainer Kit & TTL IC Guidelines
• Power Connections: Always connect Pin 14 to +5 V and Pin 7 to GND on all 14-pin 74xx TTL ICs.
• 7402 NOR Pinout Note: Unlike 7408/7432/7400/7486 (where pins 1, 2 are inputs and 3 is output), IC 7402 has Pin 1 as Output and Pins 2, 3 as Inputs.
