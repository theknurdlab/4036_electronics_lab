Decade Reset Principle (Modulo-10)
  • Asynchronous Ripple Clocking: External CLK drives FF-A (Pin 1); QA → CLKB, QB → CLKC, and QC → CLKD trigger on negative (falling) edges.
  • Truncating MOD-16 to MOD-10: All J, K, and PR inputs are tied HIGH (+5 V). On the 10th clock pulse, the transient state 10102 (QD=1, QB=1) forces the reset NAND gate (Y = QB · QD = 0) LOW, asserting active-low CLR = 0 to immediately clear all flip-flops to 00002.
