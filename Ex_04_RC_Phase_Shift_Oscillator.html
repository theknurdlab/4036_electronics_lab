BJT RC PHASE SHIFT OSCILLATOR SCHEMATIC BC548 • 3-Section RC
Barkhausen Criterion & Design Equations 3-Stage RC Network
  • Total 360° Loop Phase Shift: The CE transistor amplifier provides 180° phase inversion, and the three cascaded RC sections each contribute 60° phase lead (3 × 60° = 180°) at fo.
  • Frequency of Oscillation:
    fo = 1 2πRC√(6 + 4k) ≈ 1 2πRC√6  where k = RC / R
  • Minimum Transistor Current Gain (Barkhausen |Aβ| ≥ 1): Since feedback attenuation is β = 1/29, the transistor requires hfe(min) = 23 + 29k + 4/k ≥ 29.
