UJT Design Equations & Negative-Resistance Principle
  • Peak-Point Firing Voltage:
    VP = η · VBB + VD = 0.65(12) + 0.7 = 8.50 V
  • Time Period & Frequency of Oscillation:
    T = R1C ln 11 − η ≈ R1C   fo = 1R1C
  • Oscillation Condition: Charging resistor R1 must lie between R1(min) = (VBB − VV)/IV and R1(max) = (VBB − VP)/IP so the load line intersects the negative-resistance region of the UJT.
